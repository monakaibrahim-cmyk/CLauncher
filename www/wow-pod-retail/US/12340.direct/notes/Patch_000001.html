World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King

General

  • This patch includes various bug fixes and updates associated with the Ruby Sanctum content.

Ruby Sanctum

  • Added the Ruby Sanctum raid beneath the Wyrmrest Temple in Dragonblight.
  • Players can enter the Ruby Sanctum through a portal inside Wyrmrest Temple.
  • The raid contains the following encounters:
  • Saviana Ragefire
  • Baltharus the Warborn
  • General Zarithrian
  • Halion the Twilight Destroyer
  • Ruby Sanctum is available in both 10-player and 25-player difficulties.
  • Both Normal and Heroic difficulties are available.

PvP

  • Added the new Baradin Hold raid encounter area to Tol Barad.
  • Baradin Hold becomes available after the release of Cataclysm.

User Interface

  • Various user interface issues have been corrected.
  • Additional tooltip and display fixes have been implemented.

Bug Fixes

  • Numerous bugs affecting quests, creatures, spells, items, and encounters have been corrected.
  • Several graphical and animation issues have been resolved.
  • Various stability improvements have been made.

The Ruby Sanctum

The Black Dragonflight's assault on the Ruby Sanctum has begun.

  • Players must defend the sanctum from the forces of the Twilight Dragonflight.
  • Halion the Twilight Destroyer serves as the final encounter.
